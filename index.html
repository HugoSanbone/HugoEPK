Biography

About the Artist

Hugo Sanbone is a Brazilian multi-instrumentalist, composer, and arranger whose work brings together jazz, commercial music, and Afro-diasporic influences. Performing on trombone, trumpet, saxophone, and accordion, his musical approach combines original arrangements, distinctive grooves, and a strong sense of rhythmic interplay.

Drawing on the traditions of jazz and contemporary music, Sanbone develops a sound that reflects his experience as a performer, composer, and ensemble leader. With more than two decades of musical experience, he has performed internationally, collaborating with artists and ensembles across jazz and Afro-diasporic music.

Music Style & Sound Profile

Genres & Focus

Style Categorization

  • Contemporary Jazz: Expressive brass leads, sophisticated harmonies, and modern modal improvisation.
  • Brazilian & Afro-diasporic Rhythms: Complex syncopation, Samba-Jazz, Baião, and Frevo grooves.
  • Brass & Ensemble Work: Rich horn section voicing across trombone, trumpet, and saxophone.
  • Original Arrangements: Versatile compositions tailored for full jazz orchestras and compact combos.

Sounds Like

Aesthetic Description

A distinctive blend of contemporary jazz, Brazilian rhythms, Afro-Brazilian musical traditions, and Afro-diasporic grooves, shaped by a modern jazz language, intricate arrangements, and dynamic ensemble interplay. The music balances composition and improvisation, bringing together diverse influences through a strong sense of groove, swing, and collective expression.
